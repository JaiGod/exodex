EXODEX

Xenobiology Survey Program — land on alien worlds, scan new lifeforms, build the database.

Source on GitHub
How to play. Pick a starter, then launch from the Orbital Lab to a planet. Walk through bio-zones (tall vegetation) to meet wild lifeforms. In battle, Scan to log a species in your ExoDex and collect DNA, weaken it, then Capture it with a pod. Gather glowing resource nodes to craft pods, MedGels and Fuel Cells, spend Research Points on upgrades, and scan more species to unlock new worlds. Each planet hides an Alpha.
Keys: WASD / arrows to move · E gather · L lure · R return to orbit · 1-6 battle actions · Space to continue.
